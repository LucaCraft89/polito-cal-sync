# 028 Test buttons: feed test, login test, and the rate rule

**Decided**

- **Feed test** `POST /api/sources/feeds/test {index}`: the existing `IcsSource` on that one feed, with `NoCache` (an unconditional GET that remembers nothing in `DATA_DIR`) and **one attempt** (no backoff loop against a struggling server; a sync still retries). Answers with counts (events in the 366-day window, cancelled left out, events it could not handle) and the three-line problem, always HTTP 200 for a result. The label is `feed #N (host)`; the address, which holds a token, is in no answer, log or error. Moodle feeds get the same hints as in a sync. The index refers to the effective list (also when `SOURCE_POLITO_ICS_URLS` locks it).
- **Login test** `POST /api/sources/polito/test`: the portal source itself (`PolitoPortalSource.fetch`): one login (about 13 requests, 1.5 s apart) and one data read, mapped with the saved squads and exam-call setting. It answers **counts only** (courses, and per part: items, events, issues) plus the problem. It uses the **saved** credentials (the page refuses to test typed, unsaved ones: "Save first"), and works before the source is switched on. **Same lockout latch as a sync (decision 018):** a refusal writes the latch; while the same username and password stay, the test answers `E_POLITO_LOGIN_BLOCKED` at once with **no request at all**, even a day later; a latch written by a sync or preview blocks the test and the other way round. MFA/extra step: reported, the form is not answered, nothing is latched. An outage is not latched.
- **Rate rule** (`src/web/gate.ts`, counted from the **start** of the last attempt, a failed one counts, a refusal costs no traffic and is not counted):
  - a feed: **once per 15 minutes per address** (keyed by a hash of the address, in memory), and **at most 10 feed tests in 15 minutes in all** (so adding many wrong addresses in a row is bounded);
  - the login: **once per 15 minutes per set of credentials** (keyed by a hash of username and password). Because the key is the credentials, fixing them lifts the limit at once; the blocked-by-latch answer neither counts nor waits;
  - one test of each kind at a time (single flight), `E_UI_TEST_BUSY`.
    Refusals are `E_UI_TEST_RATE_LIMITED` (429) and `E_UI_TEST_BUSY` (409), both in `docs/ERRORS.md`.
- **Deliberately not shared with Preview.** A login test followed by a preview costs two logins in a short time. Sharing one budget would make "Test login, then Preview" in the setup flow fail for 15 minutes, which is worse; the owner's earlier live checks also made two logins. Worst case from the UI: one login test and one preview per 15 minutes.
- Tests never reach the real portal: `test/web-checks.test.ts` uses the invented portal (`portal-world.ts`) or the offline portal of `previewWorld()`; the browser test uses the invented portal and asserts the offline one was never needed.

**Verified how.** `test/web-checks.test.ts` (17 cases: session and CSRF, counts, no address or password or course name in any answer, log or audit, no cache written, one attempt on 503, the rate rule with an injected clock, the global cap, single flight, bad indexes and bodies, the latch incl. 24 hours later and with changed credentials, a latch written elsewhere, MFA, outage, partial data) and a Playwright test on a 360 px phone (feed ok, repeated test refused with no request, unsaved credentials refused, login ok with counts, repeat refused with no request, layout and axe in light and dark). Not run live against the real portal for this step: the login client and source are the ones already verified live (decisions 018, 026).
